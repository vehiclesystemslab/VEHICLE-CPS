# ETHICS.md
## VEHICLE-CPS — Civil Protection Systems

VEHICLE-CPS is designed as a civil protection architecture for environmental safety, relational coherence and controlled response in high-density human events.

It is not designed for political surveillance, profiling, autonomous enforcement or automatic coercive action.

---

## 1. Human Supervision Is Mandatory

VEHICLE-CPS may support detection, classification and prioritization, but final interpretation and action must remain under authorized human supervision.

The system should assist trained human teams. It should not become the final authority over people, movement, access or response.

---

## 2. No Automatic Coercive Action

VEHICLE-CPS must not automatically trigger force, detention, exclusion, pursuit, punishment or coercive measures.

Any operational action must be decided by authorized human personnel according to law, institutional protocols and applicable civil protection standards.

---

## 3. No Facial Recognition by Design

The initial VEHICLE-CPS research architecture does not require facial recognition.

Its focus is relational coherence, operational nodes, routes, zones, access points, credentials, teams and tension patterns — not biometric identity recognition.

---

## 4. Data Minimization

Only data necessary for civil protection and operational safety should be used.

Unnecessary personal data should not be collected, stored or processed. Data collection must be proportional to the civil protection objective.

---

## 5. Pseudonymized Operational IDs

Where possible, smart bands, credentials or tags should use pseudonymized operational identifiers rather than exposed personal identities.

The system should prioritize event-level coherence and operational safety over individual-level exposure.

---

## 6. No Political Profiling

VEHICLE-CPS must not be used to classify people by political opinion, ideology, ethnicity, religion, union activity, protest participation or protected personal attributes.

The architecture is not intended for political surveillance, social scoring or discriminatory classification.

---

## 7. Auditability

Alerts, classifications and recommendations should be logged for post-event audit, accountability and methodological improvement.

Any use of the system in operational contexts should maintain traceable records of:

- input assumptions;
- model configuration;
- alert thresholds;
- P0–P6 classification changes;
- human decisions;
- post-event review outcomes.

---

## 8. Civil Protection Scope

VEHICLE-CPS is limited to civil protection, event safety, emergency response, controlled evacuation, perimeter coherence and environmental safety use cases.

Any deployment outside this scope requires separate legal, ethical and institutional review.

---

## 9. Human Dignity

The system should protect human life, reduce harm and support safe coordination without dehumanizing participants in an event.

People should not be treated as threats by default. VEHICLE-CPS reads environmental coherence and relational tension; it does not assign guilt.

---

## 10. Institutional Review

Any physical pilot should require legal, ethical, operational and institutional review before deployment.

A controlled pilot should include:

- clear purpose limitation;
- informed institutional authorization;
- data minimization plan;
- privacy review;
- human supervision protocol;
- emergency override procedures;
- post-event audit.

---

## Closing Statement

VEHICLE-CPS is designed around prevention, not persecution.

Its purpose is to help authorized teams detect loss of environmental coherence and coordinate safer responses under explicit human authority and ethical constraints.
