# ROADMAP.md
## VEHICLE-CPS — Civil Protection Systems

This roadmap defines the staged development path for VEHICLE-CPS as a civil protection research architecture for environmental safety, relational coherence and controlled human response.

---

## Phase 0 — Conceptual Consolidation

**Goal:** establish a clear scientific, ethical and institutional foundation.

Tasks:

- refine README, ethics and scientific positioning;
- define P0–P6 operational regimes;
- clarify scope, non-scope and ethical boundaries;
- prepare Zenodo v1.1 PDF;
- prepare web section and visual identity;
- align GitHub and Zenodo records.

Deliverables:

- `README.md`;
- `ETHICS.md`;
- `ROADMAP.md`;
- Zenodo v1.1 paper;
- updated web description;
- initial partnership language.

---

## Phase 1 — Synthetic Simulation

**Goal:** validate the core architecture with synthetic human-node simulations.

Simulation targets:

- 1,000 nodes;
- 5,000 nodes;
- 10,000 nodes.

Technical tasks:

- dynamic graph construction;
- node state initialization;
- E.I.A.R.(V) semantic mapping;
- external tension model;
- internal incoherence model;
- projected correction operator;
- P0–P6 classification;
- synthetic scenario generation;
- basic visualization.

Deliverables:

- simulation engine;
- reproducible examples;
- tension maps;
- P0–P6 transition logs;
- anomaly timing measurements;
- return-to-safe-state metrics;
- technical report.

---

## Phase 2 — Operational Scenario Modeling

**Goal:** test VEHICLE-CPS on structured civil protection scenarios.

Potential scenarios:

- stadium event;
- concert crowd flow;
- protected access area;
- emergency drill;
- evacuation route pressure;
- perimeter coherence loss;
- high-emotion public event;
- critical infrastructure access environment.

Tasks:

- define scenario templates;
- create synthetic event layouts;
- define route and access constraints;
- simulate crowd pressure;
- simulate operational node disconnection;
- evaluate P0–P6 transitions;
- generate scenario reports.

Deliverables:

- scenario templates;
- KPI dashboard prototype;
- anomaly detection timing report;
- response-time simulation;
- environment coherence score;
- controlled response report.

## Phase 4 — Controlled Pilot Design

**Goal:** prepare a controlled pilot under strict legal, ethical and operational supervision.

Tasks:

- define pilot scope;
- define data minimization plan;
- define human supervision protocol;
- define operational safety checklist;
- define emergency override procedures;
- define post-event audit process;
- review legal and institutional constraints.

Deliverables:

- pilot protocol;
- privacy framework;
- operational checklist;
- human supervision protocol;
- safety and compliance review;
- controlled pilot proposal.

---

## Long-Term Research Direction

Future research may include:

- multi-scale crowd tension modeling;
- graph-based civil protection dashboards;
- privacy-preserving operational IDs;
- controlled evacuation dynamics;
- real-time environmental coherence indices;
- integration with existing event safety systems;
- partner-led validation datasets;
- ethical AI governance frameworks for civil protection.

---

## Development Principle

VEHICLE-CPS must advance through simulation, validation and review before any physical pilot.

The project prioritizes scientific rigor, institutional trust, ethical boundaries and human-supervised decision support.
